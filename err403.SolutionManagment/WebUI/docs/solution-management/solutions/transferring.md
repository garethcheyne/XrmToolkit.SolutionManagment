## Selecting Solutions

Click on a row in the solutions grid to select it. The status bar at the bottom shows how many solutions are selected.

Use the **search bar** to filter solutions by unique name, display name, or publisher.

## Starting a Transfer

With solutions selected and at least one target connected:

1. Click **Transfer** in the toolbar
2. The **Pre-Import Summary** dialog opens
3. Review and adjust settings
4. Click **Transfer** to begin

## Multi-Target Transfer

When you have multiple targets connected, each transfer operation processes every solution against every target. For example:

- 2 solutions × 4 targets = 8 transfer operations

Each operation runs sequentially: export → import → publish.

## Context Menu

Right-click any solution to access:

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Open in Maker Portal** | Opens the solution in Power Apps Maker Portal (requires authentication) |

> [!NOTE]
> The "Open in Maker Portal" option is disabled until you click **Authenticate** in the connection bar.
