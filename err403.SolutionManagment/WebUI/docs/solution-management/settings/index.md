## Overview

Click the **Settings** button in the Solutions toolbar to open the settings drawer. Settings are organized into five sections:

- **General** — Auto-save, refresh interval, notifications
- **Export** — Managed/unmanaged, export sub-components
- **Import** — Import mode, dependencies, customizations
- **Publish** — Post-import publishing
- **Version** — Version update policy and date mask

## Settings Scope

Settings are saved **per connection** — each environment gets its own settings that reload automatically when you reconnect.

Within a connection, you can also create **per-solution profiles** that override the defaults for specific solutions.

> [!TIP]
> See [Per-Solution Profiles](./solution-profiles.md) for details on customizing settings per solution.
